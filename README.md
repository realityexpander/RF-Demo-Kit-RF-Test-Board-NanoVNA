# NanoVNA RF Demo Kit – English Manual

An English-translated version of the RF Demo Kit instruction manual, originally designed by BH5HNU for learning and experimenting with vector network analyzers (VNAs).

## Contents

The 20-page manual covers 18 RF test circuits on the 10 × 10 cm demonstration PCB, including:

- RLC, RC and LC circuits
- 33 Ω and 75 Ω resistance and SWR measurements
- Capacitance and inductance measurements
- Ceramic band-pass and notch filters
- Low-pass and high-pass filters
- Short, Open, Load and Thru (SOLT) calibration
- 3 dB and 10 dB attenuation circuits
- Smith chart interpretation and impedance matching
- S11 and S21 measurements and recommended frequency ranges
